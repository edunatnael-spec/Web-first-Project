Welcome to the official Ethiopian Fighting Championship (EFC) fighter registration page. This form is designed for athletes who are passionate about combat sports and are ready to compete at a professional level. Before registering, please carefully review all the requirements and ensure that you meet the eligibility criteria listed below, as this will help us maintain fairness, safety, and high competition standards across all matches.

Requirements

In order to participate in the championship, all applicants must meet the following conditions and must be prepared to provide valid proof where necessary during the verification process conducted by the EFC organization.

  1. You must be at least 18 years old at the time of registration to ensure legal eligibility and physical maturity for competitive fighting.
  2. You must have prior training or experience in MMA, boxing, wrestling, or any recognized combat sport to ensure safety and competitiveness.
  3. You must be physically fit and medically cleared to participate in high-intensity combat sports activities.
  4. You must agree to follow all EFC rules, regulations, and code of conduct during training and official matches.
